What is Forex?

Forex is the market where people trade one currency for another, like euros for US dollars.

It is the largest financial market in the world.

What is a pip?

0.0001

A pip is the smallest usual price move. For pairs with the yen, a pip is 0.01.

Trading sessions

Sydney Tokyo London New York

The market moves most when two sessions overlap, like London and New York.

How to read a currency pair

EUR/USD = 1.0842
Base currency (first)EUR
Quote currency (second)USD
Meaning1 euro = 1.0842 dollars
BuyExpect the euro to get stronger
SellExpect the euro to get weaker

Example: if the price moves from 1.0842 to 1.0872, it went up 30 pips.

Risk rules

Always use a stop loss to limit how much you can lose.

Risk only a small part of your money on each trade, like 1% to 2%.

Leverage can grow your profits, but it also grows your losses.

Protect your money first. Profit comes second.
